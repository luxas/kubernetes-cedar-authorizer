# Properties: PLAN 3.1 clauses to Lean theorems to KEP properties

Generated by hand at stage 2; counts and axioms are in `docs/status.md` (written by
`scripts/check.sh`). KEP property numbers are from `kubernetes/review/properties.md`
(review branch `impl-conditional-authz-review`, `2ee49d340c9`). All Lean names live in
`lean/K8sAuthz` (namespace `K8sAuthz`; mirror bridge in `K8sAuthz.Bridge`).

Status values: **proved** (Lean theorem, no `sorry`, standard axioms only), **bridged**
(proved over the mirror's real types), **hypothesis** (named `Prop` or theorem argument),
**out of scope** (not a Lean statement, or deferred to a later stage).

Architecture. The core is proved once over a generic condition-map type `M`
(`Spec/Core.lean`, `Spec/Authorizer.lean`, `Spec/Chain.lean`, ...). `Bridge.lean` and
`BridgePeval.lean` instantiate `M := authorizer.ConditionsMap` and translate the mirror's
`ConditionsAwareDecision` with `toCAD`.

## Mirror theorems (`lean/mirror`) and their relation

Corollaries of the new layer (restated, over real types): P-37 (`sem_toCAD` plus `sem_mem`),
P-27, P-28, P-25 (`peval_is_refinement` composes them with `refines_sem`). Go-specific, stay in
the mirror: P-34/P-42 (name validation), P-47 (error propagation), P-49/P-52 (webhook shape), P-53
(UID correlation), P-67..P-72 (enforcer and filter composition), P-61, P-80, P-81 (feature gate,
version skew), the whole of J (CEL evaluator).

## Not bridged to the mirror (stage 2)

The mirror's `unionAuthzHandler`, webhook authorizer and enforcer are not shown equal to the
spec's `chain2`, a webhook `Authorizer` instance, or `served`. The bridge covers decisions
(`toCAD`, `pd`, `cua`, `FD`, `isUn`, `wf`, `good`, `sem`) and the partial evaluator (A-3).

## Unfinished

  of member names across all members is not part of `NamesOkN`.
- Chain domain is the conjunction `DA ∧ DB`; the weaker `DA ∧ (full_A = NoOpinion → DB)` is not
  proved.
- Error nil-ness and reason text (M3 of review A) are projected away; A-7 is a hypothesis.
- Cedar instantiation (C-1..C-9) is stage 3.
